Hair Botox Near Kothanur, Bangalore

Hair that feels like straw after colouring, heat styling or humid weather does not always need straightening. It needs repair. Hair botox rebuilds softness and shine without changing your natural texture, and our salon is five minutes from Kothanur.

What the treatment does

Hair botox is a non-chemical conditioning treatment built on proteins, vitamins, amino acids and nourishing oils. It works on the inside of the strand, so hair feels softer and behaves better.

Is it right for you?

  • Rough, frizzy hair that tangles easily
  • Dull strands that have lost their shine
  • Hair worn down by colour or heat tools
  • Anyone who wants smoother hair but not a straight finish

If you do want a straighter, sleeker result, compare it with hair smoothening or a keratin treatment.

What you get

  • Visibly less frizz
  • Moisture back in dry lengths
  • Better shine and smoothness
  • Repair for heat and colour damage
  • Safe on coloured, treated hair

Botox or keratin?

Botox repairs and hydrates; keratin straightens and smooths. If your priority is healthier hair that still looks like yours, botox is the better fit. Our keratin vs botox guide breaks the differences down, and you can also see how hair smoothening compares.

Your session

We cleanse the hair, apply the botox, activate it with heat, then rinse and blow-dry. Plan for 2 to 2.5 hours. Results typically last 5 to 6 months with good home care.

After your treatment

  • Wash with a sulphate-free shampoo
  • Go easy on heat styling
  • Skip heavy oiling
  • Use the products your stylist recommends

A regular hair spa keeps hydration up and helps results last longer.

Common questions from Kothanur

Will hair botox make my hair straight?

No. It softens frizz and smooths the surface, but your natural wave or curl pattern stays.

Can I get botox on coloured hair?

Yes. It suits coloured and chemically treated hair, and is often chosen to repair damage from colouring.

How often can I repeat it?

It can be repeated safely. Most clients come back every 5 to 6 months, depending on how they look after their hair.

What should I use to wash my hair afterwards?

A sulphate-free shampoo, ideally one meant for botox or keratin aftercare.

Yes, we are about five minutes away on Hennur Main Road.
