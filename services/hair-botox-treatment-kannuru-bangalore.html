Hair Botox in Kannuru, Bangalore

Hair botox restores moisture and smoothness to hair that has been through too much heat, colour or humidity, and it does so without turning your waves straight. You can get it at our Hennur Main Road salon in Kannuru.

In short

A deep-conditioning formula of proteins, vitamins, amino acids and oils repairs the hair from within, so it feels softer and behaves better.

Signs it could help

  • Rough, tangly hair
  • Dullness and lost shine
  • Damage from colour or heat styling
  • A wish for smoothness without straightening

For a straighter result, see hair smoothening or keratin.

Benefits

  • Noticeably less frizz
  • Deep hydration
  • More shine and softness
  • Safe for coloured hair

Process

  • Cleanse
  • Apply botox
  • Heat activation
  • Rinse and blow-dry

Expect 2 to 2.5 hours, with results lasting 5 to 6 months given proper care.

Botox or keratin?

Botox is about repair; keratin is about straightening. Compare them in our blog.

Maintenance

  • Sulphate-free shampoo
  • Less heat
  • No heavy oiling
  • Recommended products only

A regular hair spa helps hold the result.

Kannuru botox FAQs

Is hair botox a chemical treatment?

It is non-chemical, built on proteins, vitamins, amino acids and oils.

Does it change my curl pattern?

No. It softens and smooths but does not permanently straighten.

How often can I repeat it?

It can be repeated safely, commonly every 5 to 6 months.

What aftercare is needed?

Sulphate-free shampoo, limited heat and a stylist-approved routine.

Yes, at our salon on Hennur Main Road in Kannuru.
