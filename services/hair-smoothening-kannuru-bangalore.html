Hair Smoothening in Kannuru, Bangalore

Smoothening is the answer when you want sleek, easy hair without the daily blow-dry battle. We offer it at our salon in Kannuru, on Hennur Main Road.

What it brings

  • Fewer flyaways
  • A smoother surface
  • Shine and softness
  • Faster styling

Suits

  • Wavy hair that is hard to manage
  • Coarse or dry hair
  • Humidity-prone hair

Steps

  • Consultation
  • Deep cleanse
  • Smoothening cream
  • Heat seal
  • Wash and style

Allow 2 to 4 hours. Treated hair stays smooth while new growth shows natural texture.

Choosing between treatments

Compare with keratin, which restores protein, or hair botox, which repairs without straightening.

Aftercare

  • Sulphate-free shampoo
  • Less heat
  • Follow our advice
  • Use a mask regularly
  • Book a hair spa now and then

Kannuru smoothening FAQs

What does smoothening actually do?

It tames frizz, adds shine and makes hair easier to manage while keeping a natural look.

Do I need to touch it up?

New growth keeps your natural texture, so many people repeat the treatment after 5 to 6 months.

Can coloured or dry hair be smoothened?

Often yes, after assessment. Very damaged hair may need repair first.

How should I wash smoothened hair?

With a sulphate-free shampoo.

Is smoothening available in Kannuru?

Yes, at our Hennur Main Road salon.
