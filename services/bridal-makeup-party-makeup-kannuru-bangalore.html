Bridal & Party Makeup in Kannuru, Bangalore

Sugarcoat Salon is based in Kannuru itself, on the first floor of Capital Market on Hennur Main Road. If you live nearby, your trial, your wedding-morning appointment and any touch-up are all a short hop from home.

For the bride

  • Classic traditional makeup
  • HD base for camera and flash
  • Engagement and reception looks
  • Pre-wedding shoot makeup
  • Hairstyle and saree draping

The salon bridal package covers HD makeup, bridal hairstyle, saree draping and lashes. See Services & Pricing for current rates.

How your appointment is structured

We begin with a chat about your outfit and jewellery. Then comes skin prep, correction, eyes, contour and highlight, lips, and a final lock-in so the look lasts. Reserve 2 to 3 hours.

For guests and party plans

Party makeup is the lighter, faster cousin of bridal. Choose a soft finish or a statement eye, and add a party hairstyle or saree draping if you like.
  • Light to full glam
  • Eye-led looks
  • Setting for hours of wear

Allow about 60 to 90 minutes.

Kannuru bridal FAQs

Can I try the look before my wedding day?

Yes. Bridal trials are offered on request, and booking one early leaves time to tweak the base, eyes and lips.

Is party makeup much quicker than bridal?

Yes. Party makeup usually takes 60 to 90 minutes, while a full bridal look takes 2 to 3 hours.

Will I be asked to pay in advance?

For makeup and hairstyling, an advance may be required to hold your date. We confirm the amount when you book.

Can you come to my home or venue?

Yes, at a higher charge that depends on distance. Salon pricing is lower.

Where exactly is the salon in Kannuru?

On the first floor of Capital Market, Hennur Main Road, near Kanakashree Housing Society, Kannuru.
