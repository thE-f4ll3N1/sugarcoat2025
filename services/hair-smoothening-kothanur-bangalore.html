Hair Smoothening Near Kothanur, Bangalore

Tired of blow-drying every morning only to see frizz return by afternoon? Smoothening gives hair a sleek, tidy finish that still looks natural. Our Hennur Main Road salon is five minutes from Kothanur. Very damaged hair may do better with hair botox first.

What you can expect

  • Far less frizz and flyaways
  • A smoother hair surface
  • Added shine and softness
  • Easier daily styling
  • A shorter drying routine

Who usually books it

  • Wavy hair that is hard to control
  • Dry, rough hair
  • Hair that puffs up in humidity
  • Anyone who wants smoother hair with less effort

How the session goes

  • Consultation about your hair
  • Deep cleansing
  • Smoothening cream applied
  • Heat sealing
  • Wash and styling

Expect 2 to 4 hours. The treated hair stays smooth permanently, while new growth keeps your natural texture. A redo after 5 to 6 months keeps everything even.

Smoothening, keratin or botox?

Smoothening is about control and a sleek finish. Keratin adds protein and leans toward repair. You can look at our keratin treatment and hair botox pages if you are comparing options.

Aftercare

  • Use sulphate-free shampoo
  • Limit heat styling
  • Follow the aftercare advice we give you
  • Use a nourishing mask regularly
  • Book periodic hair spa sessions to keep smoothness and hydration up

Smoothening questions from Kothanur

What is the difference between smoothening and keratin?

Smoothening controls frizz and makes hair easier to manage while keeping a natural look. Keratin adds protein back into the hair and tends to focus more on repair.

Will treated hair go back to normal?

Treated hair stays smooth, but new growth comes in with your original texture. A touch-up after 5 to 6 months evens things out.

Can I smoothen hair that has been coloured?

Yes, after a professional assessment of its condition.

Is it suitable for dry hair?

Dry hair can be treated, though we may recommend a repair treatment first.

Can I visit from Kothanur easily?

Yes. We are about five minutes away on Hennur Main Road.
