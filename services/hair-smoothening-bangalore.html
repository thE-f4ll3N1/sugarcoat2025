Hair Smoothening in Bangalore

Smoothening gives hair a calm, glossy finish that is easy to style. Our stylists at Hennur Main Road, North Bangalore, assess your hair before they begin.

Why it helps

  • Less frizz
  • Smoother surface
  • Added shine
  • Faster styling

Suits

  • Wavy, difficult hair
  • Dry hair
  • Humid-weather frizz

Steps

  • Consultation
  • Cleanse
  • Smoothening cream
  • Heat seal
  • Wash and style

2 to 4 hours.

Smoothening, keratin or botox?

Smoothening controls frizz and shine, keratin adds protein, and hair botox repairs without straightening.

Aftercare

  • Sulphate-free shampoo
  • Less heat
  • Regular masks
  • A hair spa now and then

Bangalore smoothening FAQs

What is smoothening?

A treatment that reduces frizz and makes hair easier to manage with a natural look.

Does it last forever?

Treated hair stays smooth, but new growth shows your natural texture, so a redo after 5 to 6 months helps.

Is it safe on coloured hair?

Yes, after assessment.

Which shampoo do I use?

A sulphate-free one.
