Bridal & Party Makeup in Bangalore

Bangalore weddings run on long days, bright lights and plenty of photographs. Our makeup studio on Hennur Main Road in North Bangalore builds bridal and party looks to hold up through all of it.

Bridal looks

  • Traditional bridal makeup
  • HD makeup for camera
  • Engagement and reception looks
  • Photoshoot makeup
  • Hairstyle and saree draping

The salon bridal package covers HD makeup, bridal hairstyle, saree draping and lashes. Groom makeup includes HD makeup and a hairstyle. Rates are on Services & Pricing.

Party makeup

A lighter option for receptions, parties and festive evenings, finished in about 60 to 90 minutes.
  • Soft to full glam
  • Eye-focused styles
  • Hair and draping add-ons

Why a trial matters

Trying the look in advance lets you test the shade, the eyes and the lip colour in daylight and under flash, so the wedding day holds no surprises.

Complete the look

Many brides also book nail extensions, a manicure and pedicure and a haircut and styling.

Day-of tips

  • Keep blotting paper handy
  • Avoid touching your face
  • Remove makeup gently
  • Cleanse and moisturise at night

Bangalore bridal FAQs

Can I book a bridal trial?

Yes, trials are available on request. It helps to do one well before the wedding.

Do you do makeup for grooms and guests?

Yes. Groom makeup and party makeup for guests are both offered.

How long does bridal makeup take?

Plan for 2 to 3 hours. Party makeup takes roughly 60 to 90 minutes.

Can you travel to my venue?

Home and auditorium services are possible at a higher charge based on distance.
