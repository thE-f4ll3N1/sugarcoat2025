Haircut & Hair Styling in Kannuru, Bangalore

Haircuts here begin with a chat about what you want and what suits your face. We are in Kannuru on Hennur Main Road and open every day, so a trim fits easily into the week.

On the menu

  • Women's and girls' cuts
  • Men's and boys' cuts
  • Beard styling
  • Fringes and bangs
  • Blow-dry, ironing and curling

Gents' cuts start at ₹300 and ladies' cuts at ₹700. Advanced cuts cost extra.

What to expect

A quick consultation, a wash if needed, a precise cut and a finish. Most visits take 30 to 60 minutes.

Why a stylist's cut is different

  • Chosen for your face shape
  • Better volume and fall
  • Easier daily upkeep
  • Balanced, tidy edges

When to visit

  • Before an event
  • For a new look
  • To shape up a grown-out cut
  • Regular maintenance every 4 to 6 weeks

At-home care

  • Trim regularly
  • Use recommended products
  • Keep styling light
  • Try something new with hair colour

For healthier ends, add a hair spa or hair botox.

Haircut FAQs, Kannuru

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when a stylist is free, though booking ahead avoids waiting.

Yes, for boys and girls.

Does styling come with the cut?

Basic styling does. Advanced styling is extra.

What do gents' haircuts cost?

Standard gents' cuts start at ₹300, and beard styling at ₹200.

What time do you open?

Every day from 10am to 8pm.
