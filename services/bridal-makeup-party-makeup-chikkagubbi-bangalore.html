Chikkagubbi brides ask us

Do you offer a trial before the wedding?

Yes, on request. It is worth doing a few weeks ahead so we can adjust the shade, finish and lip colour with time to spare.

Will the makeup last through a long wedding day?

Professional products and careful setting are used so the look holds through the ceremony, photos and the reception.

What is the difference between bridal and party makeup?

Bridal is the full, detailed look and takes longer. Party makeup is lighter and quicker, suited to guests and evening events.

Is a deposit needed to confirm a booking?

An advance may be asked for makeup and hairstyling bookings. We confirm the amount when you message us for your date.

How far is the salon from Chikkagubbi?

It is a short five-minute trip to Hennur Main Road.
