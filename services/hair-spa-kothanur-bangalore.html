Hair Spa Near Kothanur, Bangalore

When hair feels dry, dull or heavy with build-up, a hair spa is the quickest way to get it feeling right again. Our stylists on Hennur Main Road, five minutes from Kothanur, treat the scalp as carefully as the lengths.

What a hair spa does for you

  • Feeds dry, tired hair
  • Calms a dry scalp
  • Softens rough texture
  • Brings back shine
  • Gives you a proper break from the day

Hair spa variants

We offer a basic spa plus options for frizz, dandruff, hair fall and bond repair (with Smartbond). Pricing for each is on our Services & Pricing page, and long hair costs a little more.

Best for

  • Dry or damaged hair
  • Hair that has been chemically treated
  • Dull, lifeless lengths
  • A flaky or dry scalp
  • Coloured hair, which pairs well with our hair colour services

How a session runs

We start by looking at your hair and scalp, then cleanse, apply a spa cream (with ampules or plex where it helps), steam according to your scalp, and rinse and dry. Allow 60 to 90 minutes, and book every 2 to 4 weeks.

Between treatments, spa is your maintenance plan

It is the easiest way to look after hair after keratin, hair botox, colour or smoothening.

Spa or botox?

A spa gives regular nourishment and scalp care. Hair botox is a deeper repair that also tackles frizz and structure. Many clients use both.

Keep the benefits going

  • Choose gentle shampoos
  • Cut back on heat styling
  • Stick to a simple weekly routine
  • Book your next spa before the last one wears off

Hair spa questions from Kothanur

How often should I book a hair spa?

Every 2 to 4 weeks suits most people. Our hair spa treatments work best when they become a regular habit.

Will a hair spa stop hair fall?

It cannot promise that, but a cleaner, better-nourished scalp supports healthier hair. We also have an anti-hairfall spa option.

Is a hair spa safe on coloured hair?

Yes. It is a gentle way to keep coloured hair soft and glossy.

Do men get hair spas too?

Yes. Basic and specialised spas are available for men and women.

How far is the salon from Kothanur?

Around five minutes, on Hennur Main Road.
