Hair Colour Near Kothanur, Bangalore

Whether you are covering greys or trying a new shade for the first time, our stylists on Hennur Main Road, five minutes from Kothanur, choose colour around your skin tone and how much upkeep you actually want.

Colour options

  • Global colour, with ammonia-free options
  • Root touch-up between full sessions
  • Highlights and lowlights
  • Balayage and ombré
  • Grey coverage
  • Beard and gents colour

Pricing depends on hair length, density and any earlier colour on your hair. Starting rates are on our Services & Pricing page, and we confirm the exact figure after seeing your hair.

Why a consultation comes first

The same shade can look very different on two heads. We look at your undertone, current colour and hair condition before mixing anything, and we do a strand test so there are no surprises.

Good reasons to book

  • Greys showing at the parting
  • A fresh look for a new season
  • Refreshing faded or patchy colour
  • Adding depth to your natural tone
  • Trying a trend without committing to a big change

What happens in the chair

We talk through the shade, test a strand, apply the colour, then wash, condition and style. Count on 1 to 3 hours.

Keeping colour fresh at home

  • Switch to a colour-safe, sulphate-free shampoo
  • Wash less often when you can
  • Condition deeply every week or two
  • Book regular hair spa sessions to protect shine
  • Use heat protection before every blow-dry or iron
  • If colour has left hair dry, our hair botox treatment helps repair it
  • Want a sleeker finish too? Look at hair smoothening

Colour questions we hear from Kothanur

Do you do a strand test first?

Yes. A strand test is part of the process, so we know how your hair will take the shade before the full application.

Can grey hair be covered with a softer shade?

Yes. Grey coverage can be done with natural-looking shades, and root touch-ups keep regrowth in check between full sessions.

My hair is damaged. Can I still colour it?

Usually yes, after we assess it. We may suggest a repair treatment first so the colour sits evenly.

How long will I be at the salon?

Between 1 and 3 hours, depending on whether you choose a root touch-up, global colour or highlights.

How close is the salon to Kothanur?

About five minutes away, on Hennur Main Road.

Ready for a colour change?

Message us with a photo of the shade you like and we will tell you what is realistic for your hair. We are on Hennur Main Road, a five-minute ride from Kothanur.
