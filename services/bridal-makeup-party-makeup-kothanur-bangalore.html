Bridal Makeup Near Kothanur, Bangalore

Wedding on the calendar, or a reception invite and just one evening to get ready? Our studio on Hennur Main Road is about five minutes from Kothanur, so the trial, the big morning and any last-minute touch-up never turn into a long trip.

Looks we do for brides

  • Classic traditional bridal makeup
  • HD base made for photos and video
  • Engagement and reception looks
  • Pre-wedding shoot makeup
  • Bridal hairstyle and saree draping

At the salon, the bridal package covers HD makeup, bridal hairstyle, saree draping and lashes. Current rates are on our Services & Pricing page.

How your bridal session runs

We start by talking through your outfit, jewellery and skin type. Skin is then prepped and the base corrected, followed by eyes, contour and highlight, and finally lips and a long-wear setting.

Allow 2–3 hours. The finish is built to last the full event.

Party makeup for a single evening

A lighter, quicker option for receptions, birthdays and cocktail nights. Pick soft glam or a bolder eye, and we keep the skin looking fresh rather than heavy.

  • Soft to full glam
  • Eye-focused looks
  • Base set for hours of wear
  • Party hairstyle and saree draping if you want them

Takes about 60–90 minutes.

Who usually books party makeup

  • Wedding guests and the bride's family
  • Anyone attending an engagement or reception
  • Birthday and festival hosts
  • Office and social event attendees

Many brides pair their makeup with nail extensions, a manicure and pedicure and a haircut or styling session in the week before the wedding.

Looking after your makeup on the day

  • Keep hands away from your face
  • Keep blotting paper in your bag
  • Take it off gently at night
  • Cleanse and moisturise afterwards

Questions from Kothanur brides

Can I book a bridal trial before the wedding?

Yes. Trials are available on request, so you can settle the look, base shade and lip colour well before the day.

How long should I block out on the wedding morning?

Plan for 2 to 3 hours for the full bridal look. Party makeup usually takes 60 to 90 minutes.

Will I need to pay in advance?

An advance may be needed for makeup and hairstyling bookings. Message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm the details for your date.

Can the makeup be done at my home or venue?

Home and auditorium services are possible at a higher charge that depends on the distance. Salon pricing is lower, and we are only about five minutes from Kothanur.

How far is the salon from Kothanur?

Around five minutes, on the first floor of Capital Market, Hennur Main Road.

Reserve your bridal or party slot

Wedding dates fill up early, so message us as soon as yours is fixed. We are on Hennur Main Road, a five-minute ride from Kothanur.
