Haircut & Hair Styling in Chikkagubbi, Bangalore

The best haircuts start with a conversation. We ask about your routine, look at your hair and face shape, then cut accordingly. Our Hennur Main Road salon is five minutes from Chikkagubbi.

What we cut and style

  • Women's cuts
  • Men's cuts and beard styling
  • Kids' cuts
  • Fringes and bangs
  • Blow-dry
  • Ironing and curling

Standard gents' cuts start at ₹300, ladies' at ₹700, and advanced cuts cost extra.

The difference a good cut makes

  • Frames your face
  • Adds volume where needed
  • Easy to style at home
  • Even, symmetrical finishing

Common reasons to visit

  • A makeover
  • A party or event
  • Workday grooming
  • Reshaping a grown-out cut
  • Keeping a current style

How it goes

A short consultation, a wash if needed, then the cut and finishing. Set aside 30 to 60 minutes, and return every 4 to 6 weeks.

Looking after the cut

  • Keep up regular trims
  • Use what your stylist recommends
  • Style lightly at home
  • Follow the styling tips we show you
  • Try a new shade with our hair colour services

For stronger ends, add a hair spa or our hair botox treatment.
